# P2 anonymous visual review

All 16 assigned pages were actually inspected: four solid views and wireframe for each of four anonymous methods, giving 64 output observations. No real-method mapping, candidate scores, or reference images were consulted. P2 has no source reference. Human usability is NOT_RUN.

The chair bodies are generally recognizable, but that does not establish the requested role of C. In back2 and seat_back4, the blue upright and orange main back repeatedly occupy opposing ends of the seating plane. In seat2 n1 and methods 2-4 of n3, the blue plane visibly lies below the orange main seat; the n3 gap is substantial. In seat2 n0 the blue plane is mostly occluded, so burial/seat use is uncertain. Seat32 more often places the blue plane near the generated seat outline, with some small offsets plausibly corresponding to a base or thickness.

Local slivers, jagged slats and diagonal filled panels occur in specific rows. These are distinguished from legitimate box, armchair, high-chair, arched-back and ladder-back styles. In particular, seat_back4 n2 Method 1 has an irregular crossing ladder/back band and a narrow dangling sliver, whereas the other three rows have more coherent arched spindle backs. No claim of exact mesh intersection, watertightness or usability is made.

The CSV retains one observation per output, actual page hashes, inspected views and uncertainty. Its timestamp is the completed-recording time of the review session, not a fabricated per-image tool timestamp. Anonymous observations must remain unchanged when mapping is later revealed.
