# Curriculum decision — recorded after complete main results

The mandatory stage is complete: three new recipes × 6000 updates, 384 scored output cells, and all 128 original gallery pages actually inspected across four reviewers. R1 is the audited reused FM_ONLY control. No main result is discarded.

Decision: execute exactly C20_40_MIX and C40_20_MIX, 6000 updates each, under H_ALL (lambda=0.25 throughout). Reuse R2_MIX_H_ALL as the simultaneous-MIX baseline without new baseline updates, sampling or scoring.

Evidence: R2 versus R1 reduces FF affected-free-face mean by 1.4627 pp at C20 and 1.3966 pp at C40; each improves 6/8 parents, and the mean sign survives removing any one parent. C40 CF and conflict I also improve in the mean. H_LATE has less convincing combined conflict/FF behavior. This justifies testing order on the H_ALL recipe, not adopting R2.

Contrary evidence: R2 C40 FF remains 1.4208 pp above START. C40 expanded2 CF worsens 2.1879 pp and I worsens 0.3906 pp versus R1, with both parents adverse. C40 I gains are concentrated in two parents; C20 I has 4 better and 4 worse parents. C40 small-angle mean is almost unchanged while 6/8 parents worsen. Full visual review shows gains and local failures under every method; secondary checks retain R2's broad extra back panel on f596 C40 n6 and R1's damaged back on 33ec C20 n6. No overall winner is declared.

Hypothesis to test: reorganizing the same task exposures into an initial single-ratio phase, the other ratio, then the exact same MIX suffix can reduce the remaining FF/internal-organization cost while preserving C compatibility and complete, varied chair shapes. Neither order is presumed easier or superior.

Both curricula must use the existing frozen R2 sample multiset, all original stochastic identities and paired OT targets. CPU preflight proves all 48,000 sample records match, total free supervision is 59,541,084 scalars, and the last 16,000 slots (2000 updates) match in exact order. Rebatching changes each update's whole-batch free denominator; this is a whole curriculum-recipe comparison, not constant per-scalar optimizer weighting.

Curriculum arms share precisely the same pool and multiset, but cannot repair or remove that initial deviation. H_ALL/H_LATE also differ in total HYBRID exposure.
