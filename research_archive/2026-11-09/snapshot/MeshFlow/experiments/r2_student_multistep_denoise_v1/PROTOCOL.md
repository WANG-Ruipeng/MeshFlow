# Fixed repetition protocol

12 frozen R2_USER_MIX RAW outputs (6 parents x 2 registered noises), final4000 S8_W512_KD student only. Reuse restored-C pre-GEO S1; execute rounds 2..5 = 48 new student forwards, zero training / teacher / generator forwards or new noise. Full original N, BF16 forward, FP32 parameters and residual addition, scale 0.35739 model-to-denoiser roundtrip each step. Restore original C bytes after each call. No intermediate GEO; original GEO_C once at endpoints S3/S5, 24 new GEO + 24 original quality evaluations. S1 / identity GEO / D1 are frozen existing baselines. Complete 8 AVMR thresholds, per-output and parent/noise pairing, all 12 images in two fixed opposite views. Original sources/assets are read-only. One GPU worker; actual first authorized call measured, no extra probes; CPU 2 workers x 2 threads after resource check. No automatic replay of unclosed calls. No default promotion, additional training or unregistered rounds. Full details and evidence in REPORT.md and registration.json.
