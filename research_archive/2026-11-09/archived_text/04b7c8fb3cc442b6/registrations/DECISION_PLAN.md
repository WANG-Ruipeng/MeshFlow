# First-stage decision plan

Frozen before any endpoint sampling or quality scores.

Primary decision: can the fixed Native/T1 + role + Geo architecture produce a more useful geometric tradeoff through condition distribution and HYBRID strategy alone? No B, teacher, readout, extra geometry objective or postprocessing is added.

Main quality evidence: CF, FF, C-conflict I and all original components; triangle degeneracy/shape; descriptive topology; all four outputs for every condition in fixed multi-view galleries. C equality is a contract, not proof of meaningful attachment. Component counts are descriptive because triangle-soup connectivity is sensitive to welding. Diversity is variation, not a larger-is-better score.

Compare each ratio separately. Each parent contributes the mean of its four registered noises, then parents receive equal weight. Preserve all per-noise and per-parent rows. Keep the original6/expanded2 C40 split. These8 parents were already observed in development and are not new held-out parents. The C20/C40 panels are different completion tasks, not a pure added-C causal intervention.

The factorial contrasts are:
- mixed-condition effect under H-all: R2 - R1
- mixed-condition effect under H-late: R4 - R3
- H-late effect at40%: R3 - R1
- H-late effect under MIX: R4 - R2
- interaction: (R4 - R3) - (R2 - R1)
Averaged main effects must not hide a sign-changing interaction. H-late also uses fewer HYBRID updates, so this compares strategies rather than isolating timing at equal HYBRID exposure.

Historical START5000 and HIST_FM6000 compare practical quality, not a single-factor causal effect. Source S/E/distances remain separate diagnostics where a trusted A20 reference exists, not a reconstruction promotion gate. No aggregate utility score or favorable-only metric subset will select a winner.

After every first-stage RAW and numerical result is complete, inspect the full gallery. Record compatibility and completeness failures with request IDs; distinguish evaluator observations from a human study. A mean improvement isolated to one parent or a new visible failure is not enough to proclaim superiority. A nondominated tradeoff may be retained as evidence without adoption.

Optional curricula: decide whether order could change the practical choice, and select one already-tested HYBRID strategy before dispatch. At most C20_40_MIX and C40_20_MIX6000 each, using the same registered MIX sample multiset and final2000 suffix. The chosen MIX baseline is reused.

Confirmation: only if proposing a concrete candidate for adoption. Register exactly one candidate/control pair, a genuinely independent training stream, and unused fixed noises before execution. Same-parent new-noise confirmation is explicitly not new-parent generalization. At most6000 updates per member; no automatic default switch.

All results, including regressions, failures, compute and missing evidence, remain in the final report.
