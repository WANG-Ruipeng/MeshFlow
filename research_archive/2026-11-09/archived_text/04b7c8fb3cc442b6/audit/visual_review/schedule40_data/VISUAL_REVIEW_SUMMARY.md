Scope: all 16 assigned requests, both original colored and neutral PNGs (32 images), six displayed methods each (96 output observations). Every image was actually viewed. The five fixed views in each sheet were inspected. Original-image hashes and per-request notes are retained. Method identities and numerical metric tables were not accessed. This is assistant qualitative review, not a blinded human study.

## Limits

No topological, intersection, exact-coordinate-retention or duplicate-surface claim is inferred from pictures. Small and occluded surfaces remain uncertain. Fixed C shapes may already contain awkward extensions. Comparisons are within matched condition/noise sheets; views share cameras and bounds as registered. Full row-level observations, including uncertainty and unfavorable cases, remain available.
