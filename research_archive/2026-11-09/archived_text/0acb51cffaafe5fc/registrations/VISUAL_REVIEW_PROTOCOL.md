# Complete gallery review protocol

Recorded before any new endpoint rollout or main-stage quality result. This is an assistant visual inspection, not a human study or certified geometry test.

Review every registered request in both colored and neutral original renderings (64 requests, 128 pages). Each page shows Input C and all six methods, in four solid views and one wire view. Cameras/bounds are shared across all methods and all four noises of the condition. No cropping, repair, smoothing or source-target rendering is introduced for this review.

Assign four disjoint condition groups to reviewers. Record request ID, both image paths and hashes, reviewed method aliases, and concise observations of: recognizable overall chair organization; obvious missing/collapsed structural regions; visibly isolated or duplicated surfaces; and whether the colored C appears coherently incorporated. Do not assume a requested semantic role for C or require matching its source completion. Exact C preservation is checked numerically; images cannot certify attachment or intersection absence.

Use visible / not evident / uncertain wording with localized observations rather than fabricated precise quality scores. For each condition compare all four noises; identify regressions and benefits by request ID. When scale or occlusion prevents judgment, record uncertainty. Every method remains in the table even when outputs are poor.

Initial reviewers should use Method aliases and avoid the private mapping and aggregate metric tables until their notes are saved, where practical. This is only reduced access to labels, not a claim of an independent blind human evaluation. Record any access to model identities. Decode aliases afterward for comparison with numerical results; retain the original notes.

Root reviews its assigned pages and rechecks important disagreement/tail cases. Merge all observations, verify coverage exactly against gallery.json, and report missing inspection honestly. Candidate or curriculum decisions follow the complete numerical and visual evidence. No selection of only attractive noise outputs and no default-model switch.
